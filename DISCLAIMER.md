# Disclaimer

Saboot is a software tool. It is **not legal advice**, it does not create a lawyer-client relationship, and it is **not the "expert"** referred to in section 63(4) of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023, or in Part B of the certificate in the Schedule.

- The documents it produces are drafts. A practising advocate should review every page before anything is filed.
- Part B of the certificate must be completed and signed by an expert who examines the records independently. The Part B that Saboot prepares is a watermarked draft for the expert's convenience, not an expert opinion.
- A matching hash value shows that two files are bit-for-bit identical. It does not show that what a file contains is true, lawful, complete or admissible.
- The law is still developing. In particular, who may sign Part B, whether a certificate is needed when the original device is produced, and whether a missing certificate can be filed later are not settled. See the "About the certificate" page in the app and [docs/research/legal.md](docs/research/legal.md).
- Device identification guides are compiled from manufacturer documentation and other sources, each marked with how well it is sourced. Menus change between models and software versions; always check on the actual device.
- The statutory text is reproduced from the Gazette of India. If it differs from the official text in force, the official text prevails.
