# Getting help

## With the app

- **Something does not work, or looks wrong:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/byjoellobo/saboot/issues/new/choose). Remove all personal data first: no real chats, phone numbers, IMEIs, names or case details.
- **A WhatsApp export Saboot cannot read:** use the "WhatsApp export format" issue form and attach a short **synthetic** sample you typed yourself.
- **A device guide step is wrong for your phone or recorder:** use the "Device guide correction" form.
- **An idea or a question about how something works:** open a feature request, or read [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).
- **A security problem:** do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## With your case

Saboot cannot give legal advice, and the maintainers cannot review your evidence or your certificate. For help with a case:

- Ask a practising advocate to review every document before it is filed.
- Free legal aid is available through the National Legal Services Authority and the State and District Legal Services Authorities: [nalsa.gov.in](https://nalsa.gov.in/).
- Part B of the certificate must be completed by an expert who examines the records independently; your advocate can advise who to approach.
